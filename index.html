💌 A Letter For You, Ashley

✨ 🍃 🌿
Happy 17th birthday, Ashley. 🤍 🐾

I hope you really enjoyed your day today. Sana naging masaya ka, kahit sa maliliit na bagay lang, and sana ma-extend pa yung happiness mo hanggang tomorrow and sa mga susunod pang araw. Deserve mong maging masaya, hindi lang ngayong birthday mo, kundi araw-araw.

I hope na kahit marami kang ginagawa at maraming bagay kang iniisip, nakakahanap ka pa rin ng time para magpahinga. Please take breaks din, okay? Hindi mo kailangang laging maging strong at kayanin lahat. Sana maalagaan mo rin yung sarili mo at mabigyan mo yung sarili mo ng pahinga.

Thank you, Ashley. Thank you kasi dahil sa'yo, naranasan ko kung paano ako mahalin, and dahil din sa'yo, natutunan ko kung paano magmahal. You taught me so much about love, patience, understanding, and caring for someone genuinely. I will always be grateful na nakilala kita at naranasan kong mahalin ka at mahalin mo rin ako.

Aminado ako, hanggang ngayon nami-miss pa rin kita. Namimiss ko yung kulit mo, yung random mong kwento, lalo na kapag bigla ka na lang napapakwento ulit tungkol sa kung ano-ano. Namimiss ko yung mga simpleng moments natin na dati parang normal lang, pero ngayon nare-realize ko kung gaano pala sila ka-special sa akin.

And dw, hindi ako manggugulo. This is only an appreciation and birthday letter. Hindi ko ito sinulat para guluhin ka, para may hingin sa'yo, or para baguhin yung birthday mo. Gusto ko lang batiin ka at magpasalamat sa'yo.

Ik, baka wala na mangyari. I mean, baka hindi na tayo maayos. Baka hindi na bumalik sa dati, baka hindi na natin maibalik yung mga bagay na dati nating meron, or baka talagang hanggang dito na lang tayo. Hindi ko naman alam kung ano ang mangyayari, and I know na hindi ko kayang kontrolin kung ano yung magiging desisyon mo.

Pero kahit gano'n, may part pa rin sa akin na umaasa na baka someday, magkaroon tayo ng pagkakataon na maging okay ulit. Kasi kung magiging honest ako, ikaw pa rin yung gusto kong maging end game. Hindi ako mamimilit, though. Ayokong maramdaman mo na kailangan mong bumalik sa akin dahil sa mga sinasabi ko o dahil sa letter na 'to.

Gusto ko lang sabihin na kung magkaroon man ulit ng pagkakataon para sa atin, gusto kong mas maging better. Mas mature, mas patient, mas understanding, at mas marunong magmahal. Gusto kong ayusin yung mga bagay na kaya kong ayusin at maging taong mas deserve mong mahalin.

Pero for now, gusto ko lang na maging happy ka.

I hope this new chapter of your life brings you more reasons to smile, more memories worth keeping, and more moments na genuinely magpapasaya sa'yo. I hope you achieve the things you're dreaming about, even the things you haven't told anyone yet.

Seventeen is just the beginning of so many things, Ashley. You still have so much ahead of you. I hope you get to experience so many beautiful things, meet people who will appreciate you, and become the person you want to be.

Thank you for everything, Ashley. Thank you for letting me experience how it feels to be loved by you. Thank you for teaching me how to love, and thank you for all the memories that I will always treasure.

I don't know what will happen to us from here. Maybe we'll find our way back to each other, maybe we won't. But whatever happens, I'll always be grateful that I got to love you and that, at one point, you loved me too.

Happy 17th birthday, Ashley. 🤍

I hope you had a beautiful day today, and sana ma-extend pa yung happiness mo hanggang tomorrow.

Please take care of yourself, okay?

I love you, and I'll always be grateful for you.

Always grateful for you,
Your Kian 🤍 🐾
